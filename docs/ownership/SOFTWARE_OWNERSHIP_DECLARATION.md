## 1. Declarant

I, **Jesse Ryan Sheffield** ("Declarant"), make this declaration concerning software projects developed under the J-ROC AI name and listed in the attached or separately maintained Software Asset Schedule.

## 2. Ownership statement

Subject to the qualifications in this document, I declare that I claim ownership, or the right to control and maintain, the original software code, original documentation, original interface designs, original configuration, original test assets, and original technical materials that I personally created or that have been validly assigned or transferred to me or to an entity I lawfully control.

This declaration is intended to document the ownership position for the J-ROC AI software portfolio. It does not itself transfer rights that are owned by another person or entity, cure a missing assignment, establish authorship of disputed material, or override a third-party license.

## 3. Covered project family

The intended portfolio includes, to the extent identified in the current Software Asset Schedule:

- **J-ROC AI Universal Repair Suite PRO** — intended unified Windows 11 x64 repair-workstation application.
- **J-ROC AI CAM PRO** — Computer Automation Management PRO, including eligible bench workflow, device discovery, diagnostics, evidence and automation components.
- **J-ROC AI Universal Repair PRO** — eligible repair-workflow and device-service components.
- **J-ROC ODIN WEB** — eligible browser dashboard and local-bridge components, where rights are held or validly assigned.
- First-party shared services, device profiles, policies, tests, installer assets, original UI assets, documentation, and build/release automation created for these projects.

Project names in this list describe intended scope, not a representation that every feature is complete, tested, or compatible with every device.

## 4. Rights intended to be retained

For covered original material in which I hold the relevant rights, I intend to retain and control the applicable rights to reproduce, modify, maintain, compile, distribute, license, commercialize, rebrand, and create derivative works, subject to law and any valid third-party obligations.

No statement here should be read as ownership of third-party trademarks, device firmware, proprietary protocols, operating systems, vendor tools, libraries, or other material that is not owned by me.

## 5. Third-party material and dependencies

The software may use operating-system interfaces, device drivers, public protocols, libraries, development tools, vendor documentation, or other third-party material. Such components remain subject to their respective ownership and license terms. A dependency inventory and applicable notices should be maintained with each release. No third-party component should be represented as first-party-owned merely because it is included in a build.

## 6. Contributors and work-for-hire

Work created by employees, contractors, collaborators, or AI-assisted development workflows must be evaluated under the applicable law and relevant agreements. Where an assignment or license is necessary, it must be executed by the actual rights holder. This declaration does not substitute for a signed assignment from a contributor.

## 7. Records and evidence

Ownership records should be supported, where available, by dated source-control history, original design/source files, signed contributor agreements, invoices or work orders, dependency and license inventories, release records, and signed versions of this declaration. Preserve the original records and do not backdate signatures or assignments.

## 8. No unsupported certification

This is a private declaration template, not a government registration, court finding, legal opinion, or independent verification of title. It does not guarantee enforceability in every jurisdiction.
